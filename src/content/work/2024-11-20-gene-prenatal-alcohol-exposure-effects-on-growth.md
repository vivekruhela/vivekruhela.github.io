---
title: "Gene × prenatal alcohol exposure effects on growth and cognition in mother-child dyads"
kind: abstract   # patent | journal | preprint | abstract | talk | tool
date: 2024-11-20
venue: "ASHG 2024"
---

Mother and child genotypes in a South African birth cohort reveal new loci that interact with prenatal alcohol exposure, such as STX6 for child height and FOXD3 for recognition memory. Methyl-donor metabolism genes linked to reduced child height (FADS1/2 and MAT2A in children; MTHFR and MTHFD2 in mothers) suggest choline supplementation could lessen the effects of exposure.
